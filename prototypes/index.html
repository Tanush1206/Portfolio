What to check
  • Click SFO from DEL — the route arcs over the Arctic, because that's genuinely the great circle. Heading reads 016°.
  • Distances and ETE are real: DEL→SFO computes 12,382 km against a published 12,376 km.
  • Watch the aircraft bank into the turn and roll out on final; contrail dissolves behind it.
  • Drag to take manual camera control — it releases automatically on the next departure.
  • Labels hide on the far side of the globe; land dots fade on the limb.
  • Resize narrow — the departures board becomes a horizontal scroller.
